# Tech Lead and Senior Engineer Interview Questions

[Back to index](index.md)

**Target:** Senior Java developer / tech lead  
**Format:** Questions only, as requested. Prepare answers from your own experience.

Public tech-lead interview guides repeatedly group questions around technical direction, architecture decisions, conflict, delivery, mentoring, incidents, prioritization, and communication. These prompts are original practice questions shaped around those themes. They intentionally contain no suggested answers.

## Career scope and leadership approach

### Q1. Tell me about your current role and the technical scope you own.

### Q2. What does technical leadership mean to you?

### Q3. How do you balance hands-on coding with technical leadership responsibilities?

### Q4. How do you decide when to lead through code and when to delegate?

### Q5. What is the most technically complex system or project you have led?

### Q6. What outcomes are you most proud of, and how did you measure their impact?

### Q7. How has your leadership approach changed as your responsibilities grew?

### Q8. What kind of team or technical environment helps you do your best work?

## Architecture and technical decisions

### Q9. Tell me about an architecture decision you led from problem discovery through delivery.

### Q10. How do you make a technical decision when requirements are incomplete?

### Q11. Tell me about a time you chose a simpler solution over a more technically impressive one.

### Q12. Describe a design decision where you had to trade performance against maintainability.

### Q13. Tell me about a technical decision you changed after new evidence became available.

### Q14. How do you document and communicate important architecture decisions?

### Q15. Describe a time you disagreed with a principal engineer, architect, or senior colleague about a design.

### Q16. How do you decide whether to build, buy, or adopt an open-source component?

### Q17. Tell me about a time you introduced a new technology or platform to a team.

### Q18. Describe a time a technology choice did not work out as expected.

### Q19. How do you keep an architecture flexible without overengineering it?

### Q20. Tell me about a system boundary or service boundary you changed. Why?

### Q21. How do you handle architectural consistency across several teams?

### Q22. Describe how you would explain a complex technical decision to a non-technical stakeholder.

## Delivery, planning, and prioritization

### Q23. Tell me about a project you delivered under a difficult deadline.

### Q24. How have you handled a project whose scope kept growing?

### Q25. Tell me about a time you had to reduce scope while protecting the most important outcome.

### Q26. How do you prioritize feature work, reliability work, and technical debt?

### Q27. Describe a time you identified a delivery risk early and changed the plan.

### Q28. Tell me about a project that missed its deadline. What happened, and what did you do?

### Q29. How do you estimate work when there are major technical unknowns?

### Q30. How do you decide whether to split a large delivery into smaller releases?

### Q31. Tell me about a time you coordinated work across teams with different priorities.

### Q32. How do you keep stakeholders informed when plans or estimates change?

### Q33. Tell me about a time you had to say no or push back on a request.

### Q34. How do you protect engineering focus when urgent work repeatedly interrupts planned work?

## Conflict, influence, and communication

### Q35. Tell me about a technical disagreement you helped resolve.

### Q36. Describe a time you changed someone's mind without having authority over them.

### Q37. Tell me about a time your recommendation was rejected. What did you do next?

### Q38. How do you respond when a team strongly disagrees with your proposal?

### Q39. Tell me about a conflict between engineering and product or business stakeholders.

### Q40. Describe a time a misunderstanding caused a project problem. How did you address it?

### Q41. How do you make sure quieter team members are heard during design discussions?

### Q42. Tell me about a time you had to give difficult feedback to a colleague.

### Q43. Describe a time you received feedback that changed how you work.

### Q44. How do you handle a team member who repeatedly disagrees in meetings but does not raise concerns earlier?

### Q45. Tell me about a time you helped two people or teams reach a shared decision.

## Mentoring and growing the team

### Q46. Tell me about someone you mentored and how you helped them grow.

### Q47. How do you adapt your mentoring style to different experience levels?

### Q48. Describe a time you helped a junior engineer take ownership of a difficult task.

### Q49. How do you review code in a way that improves quality without discouraging the author?

### Q50. Tell me about a time you raised the engineering quality of a team.

### Q51. How do you identify and address a skill gap across a team?

### Q52. How do you support a high-performing engineer who wants more responsibility?

### Q53. Tell me about a time you had to address repeated quality or delivery problems with a teammate.

### Q54. How do you prevent a team from depending too heavily on one technical expert?

### Q55. Tell me about a process or practice you changed to help the team work better.

## Production incidents and reliability

### Q56. Tell me about a serious production incident you led or helped resolve.

### Q57. How do you act when the root cause of an incident is not yet known?

### Q58. Describe a time you had to choose between restoring service quickly and collecting more diagnostic evidence.

### Q59. Tell me about an incident caused by a technical decision you made.

### Q60. How do you run a blameless post-incident review?

### Q61. Tell me about a recurring incident and how you stopped it from recurring.

### Q62. How do you decide which reliability improvements to do first?

### Q63. Describe how you communicate during an incident to engineers, managers, and customers.

### Q64. Tell me about a time you improved observability or operational readiness.

### Q65. How do you balance delivery speed against reliability and operational risk?

## Failure, ambiguity, and learning

### Q66. Tell me about a technical decision you regret.

### Q67. Describe a project that failed to achieve its intended outcome.

### Q68. Tell me about a time you made a mistake that affected another team or customer.

### Q69. How do you make progress when the problem is ambiguous and there is no clear owner?

### Q70. Tell me about a time you had to learn an unfamiliar technology quickly to help your team.

### Q71. Describe a time your first diagnosis of a production problem was wrong.

### Q72. Tell me about a time you asked for help or escalated a risk earlier than expected.

### Q73. How do you decide when to keep investigating and when to make a reversible decision?

### Q74. What have you changed in your leadership approach after a failure?

## Ownership, customer impact, and ethics

### Q75. Tell me about a time you improved a system because of customer feedback.

### Q76. Describe a time you balanced customer needs against technical constraints.

### Q77. Tell me about a risk you raised that others initially dismissed.

### Q78. How do you respond when asked to ship something you believe is unsafe or unreliable?

### Q79. Tell me about a time you protected user data or improved security despite delivery pressure.

### Q80. Describe a situation where the technically correct choice conflicted with an organizational preference.

### Q81. Tell me about a time you took ownership of a problem outside your team's formal scope.

### Q82. How do you make sure engineering decisions consider accessibility, privacy, and long-term support?

## Senior/lead role judgment

### Q83. What would you do if a team repeatedly missed commitments because its estimates were unreliable?

### Q84. What would you do if a critical service had one engineer who understood how it worked?

### Q85. How would you respond if a team wanted to rewrite a stable system using a new technology?

### Q86. How would you handle a high-impact project where the product goal changes every few weeks?

### Q87. What would you do if a senior engineer consistently produced technically strong work but harmed team collaboration?

### Q88. How would you handle pressure to add a feature that creates a known security or reliability risk?

### Q89. How would you decide whether a team should adopt a shared platform or keep local ownership?

### Q90. What would you focus on in your first 90 days as a tech lead?

### Q91. How would you know whether your team is becoming more effective?

### Q92. What would you do if the team's architecture is blocking delivery but a rewrite is too risky?

### Q93. How would you handle a critical deadline when the team is already at capacity?

### Q94. How would you respond if your manager and product partner gave conflicting priorities?

### Q95. What questions would you ask before taking technical ownership of a mature production system?
