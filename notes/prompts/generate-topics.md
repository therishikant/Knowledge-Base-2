IMPORTANT RULES
==================================================

1. Think like a curriculum designer, not a search engine.

2. Do not simply list everything related to the topic.

3. Organise concepts according to their prerequisites and dependencies.

4. Start simple and gradually increase complexity.

5. Cover BOTH:
   - knowledge needed to work effectively with the technology
   - knowledge needed for interviews

6. Clearly distinguish essential knowledge from specialised knowledge.

7. Include practical examples where they improve understanding.

8. Include internal concepts when they are important for understanding how the technology works.

9. Include common interview traps and misconceptions.

10. Include senior/architect-level knowledge, but clearly separate it from beginner/intermediate material.

11. Don't overwhelm the learner with unnecessary implementation details too early.

12. If the topic is extremely broad, divide it into logical modules rather than leaving anything important out.

13. Do not teach the topics yet.
First create the COMPLETE roadmap.

14. After generating the roadmap, ask me whether I want to start learning from Level 0/Phase 1.
