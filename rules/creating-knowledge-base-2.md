# Creating Knowledge Base 2

## What I want to create
I want to create this knowledge base. It has areas where I can learn using the LLM and create notes. It also has a plan area.

## The three areas
There are three areas:
1. Plan
2. Learn
3. Notes

## Permissions
- The Plan and Notes areas are read-only for the LLM. The LLM cannot modify these two areas.
- The LLM can only modify in the Learn section.
- Enforcement is hard: the setup technically prevents the AI from writing to those areas.

## Purpose of each area
- **Plan**: Everything I intend to learn about.
- **Learn**: A place where the AI will help me learning, and also revision of the old concepts that I have learned.
- **Notes**: I will only create the notes. The AI can verify whether the notes I have written are correct or not and will give suggestions, but will not change anything in the Notes area. It can suggest me, but I will be the one who will change anything in the Notes area.

## Reason behind the read-only part
The reason behind the read-only part is so that I become more engaged in it. For the Notes area, I will be responsible for creating notes so that everything gets mapped in my brain, and it will also make me understand better as I write.

- This is just a repo in which I will learn and revise new concepts.
